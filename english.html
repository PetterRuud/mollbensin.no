Møll Bensinstasjon / YX Holum is a gas station located in Holum, about 8 km north of Mandal. Along with gas and snacks, you can also buy outdoor power products like lawn mowers, chainsaws, trimmers and more.

Adapted opening hours

Location and directions

We are located in Holum, about 8 km north of Mandal, along road 455. Get driving directions from your location to Møll Bensinstasjon.

Fishing cards for salmon fishing in Mandalselva

Fishing cards can now be bought at inatur.no. Before you start fishing, the fishing gear must be disinfected (including waders, nets, and anything else that will come into contact with the river). This can be done with us for a fee of 50 NOK. A receipt will be issued once the fishing gear is disinfected, and you must keep it in case of an inspection.
